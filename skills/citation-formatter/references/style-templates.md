# Citation style templates

## APA 7th edition

**Book:** Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of work: Capital letter also for subtitle*. Publisher.

**Journal article:** Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. *Journal Name*, *Volume*(Issue), pages. https://doi.org/xxxx

**Website:** Author, A. A. (Year, Month Day). *Title of page*. Site Name. URL

- In-text: (Author, Year) or (Author, Year, p. X) for direct quotes.
- 3+ authors: cite first author + "et al." from the first citation onward.
- Reference list: alphabetical by first author surname, hanging indent.

## MLA 9th edition

**Book:** Author Last, First. *Title of Book*. Publisher, Year.

**Journal article:** Author Last, First. "Title of Article." *Journal Name*, vol. X, no. X, Year, pp. XX-XX.

**Website:** Author Last, First. "Title of Page." *Site Name*, Day Month Year, URL.

- In-text: (Author page#) — no comma, no "p."
- Works Cited list: alphabetical by author surname, hanging indent.
- No DOI/URL access date required by default (only if the source is otherwise undated or unstable).

## Chicago 17th edition (two systems — ask which the user needs)

**Notes-Bibliography (humanities):**
Footnote: Author First Last, *Title of Book* (Place: Publisher, Year), page.
Bibliography: Author Last, First. *Title of Book*. Place: Publisher, Year.

**Author-Date (sciences/social sciences):**
In-text: (Author Year, page)
Reference list: Author Last, First. Year. *Title of Book*. Place: Publisher.

## IEEE

In-text: numbered, in order of first citation — [1], [2], etc.

Reference list entry: [1] A. Author, "Title of paper," *Journal Name*, vol. X, no. X, pp. XX-XX, Month Year.

- References are numbered in citation order, not alphabetical.

## Harvard (common variant — note that "Harvard" isn't a single fixed standard; confirm the institution's specific guide if precision matters)

In-text: (Author, Year)

Reference list: Author, A.A., Year. *Title of book*. Place: Publisher.
